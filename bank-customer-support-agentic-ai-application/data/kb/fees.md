# Fee Schedule (Personal Accounts)

## Monthly maintenance fees
Everyday Checking has a $12 monthly maintenance fee. The fee is waived when you keep a $1,500 minimum daily balance, receive $500 or more in direct deposits per statement cycle, or are under 25 years old. High-Yield Savings has no monthly maintenance fee.

## Overdraft and returned items
The overdraft fee is $25 per item, charged at most 3 times per business day. We do not charge an overdraft fee when the account is overdrawn by $50 or less at the end of the business day. The non-sufficient funds (returned item) fee is $0 — we eliminated NSF fees in 2024. Overdraft Protection transfers from a linked savings account are free.

## ATM fees
Horizon Trust ATMs are free for all customers. Using an out-of-network ATM costs $2.50 per withdrawal; the ATM owner may charge an additional fee. Premier Checking customers receive up to 4 out-of-network ATM fee refunds per month.

## Wire transfer fees
Incoming domestic wires: $15. Outgoing domestic wires: $25 ($0 for Premier Checking). Outgoing international wires: $45 when sent in US dollars and $0 when sent in foreign currency (an exchange rate margin applies).

## Other fees
Stop payment: $30. Cashier's check: $10. Replacement debit card: free for standard delivery, $25 for expedited (2-business-day) delivery. Paper statements: $2 per month (e-statements are free).
