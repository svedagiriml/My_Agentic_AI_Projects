# Disputing a Transaction and Fraud Protection

## Reporting unauthorized transactions
Report an unauthorized debit card or electronic transaction as soon as possible. Under Regulation E, your liability is limited to $50 if you report within 2 business days of learning of the loss, and up to $500 if you report within 60 days of your statement. Our Zero Liability policy covers you for unauthorized card transactions reported promptly.

## Investigation timeline
We will investigate and resolve most disputes within 10 business days. If we need more time (up to 45 days, or 90 days for new accounts, foreign or point-of-sale transactions), we will provide a provisional credit within 10 business days for eligible claims while we investigate.

## How we contact you
Horizon Trust will never ask for your full card number, PIN, password or one-time passcode by phone, e-mail or text. If you receive such a request, do not respond — call the number on the back of your card.
