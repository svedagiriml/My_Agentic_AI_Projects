# Savings Accounts and Certificates of Deposit

## High-Yield Savings
High-Yield Savings earns a variable rate with no minimum balance. Interest compounds daily and is credited monthly. Federal Regulation D transaction limits no longer apply, but we allow up to 6 free outgoing transfers per month.

## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
CDs are available with terms from 3 to 60 months with a minimum opening deposit of $500 (3- and 6-month terms) or $1,000 (12 months and longer). The APY is fixed for the term. At maturity there is a 10-day grace period to withdraw or change terms without penalty; otherwise the CD renews for the same term at the current rate.

## Early withdrawal penalty
Early withdrawal from a CD incurs a penalty of 90 days' interest for terms of 12 months or less, and 180 days' interest for terms longer than 12 months.

## Deposit insurance
Deposits are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category. Investment products such as mutual funds are not FDIC insured.
