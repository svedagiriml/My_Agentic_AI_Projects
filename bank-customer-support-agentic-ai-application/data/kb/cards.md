# Debit and Credit Card Services

## Freezing a card
You can temporarily freeze and unfreeze your card at any time in the mobile app or by asking support. While frozen, new purchases and ATM withdrawals are declined, but recurring payments you set up before the freeze may still be processed.

## Lost or stolen cards
Report a lost or stolen card immediately. The card is permanently blocked and cannot be reactivated. A replacement card arrives in 5-7 business days with standard delivery, or in 2 business days with expedited delivery.

## ATM withdrawal limits
The default daily ATM withdrawal limit is $500 for Everyday Checking debit cards and $1,000 for Premier Checking. You can request a limit of up to $5,000 per day; increases above $2,000 are reviewed by our team.

## Travel
You don't need to set a travel notice, but please keep your phone number up to date so we can reach you if we see unusual activity.
