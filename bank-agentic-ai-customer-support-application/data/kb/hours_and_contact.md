# Branch Hours, Contact and Holidays

## Branch hours
Most Horizon Trust branches are open Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM local time. Branches are closed on Sundays. Hours for an individual branch are available through the branch locator.

## Customer service
Phone banking is available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0199. Card lost or stolen line (24/7): 1-800-555-0123. Live chat agents are available 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM Eastern Time every day.

## Federal holidays
Branches and the customer care centre's account-servicing teams are closed on Federal Reserve holidays. Transfers scheduled on a holiday are processed the next business day. ATMs, mobile and online banking remain available.
