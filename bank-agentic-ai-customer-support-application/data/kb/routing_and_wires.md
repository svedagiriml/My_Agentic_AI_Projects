# Routing Number, Direct Deposit and Wire Transfers

## Routing number
Horizon Trust Bank's ABA routing number for ACH, direct deposit and domestic wires is 071000990. The same routing number is used in all states. Your account number is shown in the mobile app under Account details.

## SWIFT code for international wires
To receive an international wire, give the sender our SWIFT/BIC code HZTBUS33XXX, the routing number and your account number.

## Wire cut-off times
Domestic outgoing wires requested before 4:00 PM Eastern Time on a business day are sent the same day. International wires requested before 3:00 PM Eastern Time are usually received in 1-3 business days, depending on the destination country and intermediary banks.

## Wire safety
Wires are generally irreversible. Never send a wire to someone you have only met online, or because someone claiming to be from the bank, the government or a utility asked you to. Horizon Trust will never ask you to move money to a "safe account".
