# Mortgages, Auto and Personal Loans

## Applying for a mortgage
You can apply online in about 20 minutes. You'll need two years of income history, your two most recent pay stubs, two months of bank statements and identification. Pre-approval decisions are usually issued within 1 business day; full underwriting typically takes 2-3 weeks.

## Rate locks
Mortgage rates can be locked for 30, 45 or 60 days at no cost once your application is submitted. A one-time float-down is available if rates fall by 0.25% or more during the lock period.

## Auto and personal loans
Auto loans are available for new and used vehicles with terms of 36-72 months; there is no prepayment penalty. Personal loans range from $2,000 to $50,000 with terms of 24-60 months.

## Fair lending
Horizon Trust is an Equal Housing Lender. Credit decisions are based on creditworthiness and never on race, colour, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age or receipt of public assistance.
