# Privacy, Security and Digital Banking

## Your data
We collect only the information needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, subject to legal record-keeping requirements (for example, transaction records are retained for 7 years). We never sell your personal information.

## Online banking security
Sign-in requires multi-factor authentication. Sessions time out after 10 minutes of inactivity. You can review and sign out of active sessions from Security settings.

## Zelle and mobile deposits
Zelle payments are typically received within minutes. The daily Zelle sending limit is $2,500. Mobile check deposits made before 9:00 PM Eastern Time are usually available the next business day; the daily mobile deposit limit is $10,000.
